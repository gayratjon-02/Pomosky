Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Hosting and joining work without one. An account only adds your personal focus statistics.

Who controls the timer?

Only the host, through a secret token that stays in their browser. Viewers just watch and react.

Where does the weather come from?

From the host's rounded location. Viewers are never asked where they are.

What if the host loses the link?

Keep the host page open or bookmarked. The token is stored in that browser so you can come back.
