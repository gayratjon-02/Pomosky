Privacy

Pomosky is built to need as little about you as possible. This page says exactly what is stored.

If you only watch or host a room

  • No account, no cookies for tracking, no advertising.
  • Viewers are never asked for their location.
  • A room lives in memory while it is active and disappears shortly after the host leaves.
  • The host's place is stored rounded to about one kilometre, only to draw the sky and weather.
  • The host key stays in the host's browser. The server only keeps a hash of it.
  • There is no free-text chat. Reactions are simple counters.
  • For the community totals on the statistics page, a room hosted without an account leaves only the time a focus phase started and how long it lasted. No room link, no name, no location and no IP address is stored with it.

If you log in (optional)

  • We store your sign-in identity (Google account id or your email), a nickname and your focus statistics: the start and end time of each focus session you hosted.
  • A session cookie keeps you logged in. It is HTTP-only and not used for tracking.
  • Your detailed statistics are visible only to you, on your account page.
  • Your public name and profile picture, and the total time you focused, appear on the public leaderboard. Your email address is never shown. You can change the name or hide yourself from the leaderboard at any time on your account page.

Deleting your data

Logged-in users can delete their account and all of its statistics from the account page. Deletion is permanent.

Technical logs

The server keeps short-lived request logs (path, status, time) for reliability and abuse prevention. They are not linked to profiles.
